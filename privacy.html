Privacy Policy

Effective June 26, 2026 · Applies to the GiVo Stream app (iOS, iPadOS, tvOS) and the GiVo Server app (macOS, Windows), published by GJ Digital LLC.

The short version: we don't collect your data. GiVo Stream has no accounts, no advertising, and no tracking of our own. Your playlists, viewing activity, and recordings stay on your devices and your own computer. The one third-party component is Google Cast, used only when you cast from an iPhone or iPad (see Casting below).

What the apps store, and where

Playlists and credentials

Playlist URLs and Xtream Codes logins you add are stored on your device. Login credentials are kept in the device Keychain, Apple's encrypted credential store. The apps connect directly from your device to the content sources you configure — your playlist details are never sent to us or to any intermediary we operate.

Guide data and viewing

Programme-guide (EPG) data is downloaded from the source you configure and cached on-device. What you watch, your favorites, and your timeshift buffer exist only on your device. We have no way to see any of it.

DVR recordings

If you use GiVo Server, recordings are written to a folder on your own Mac or PC and are streamed only to the devices you pair with it over your network. Device pairing uses a TLS connection with certificate pinning; the pairing token is stored in your device's Keychain. You can revoke any device's access at any time from the app or the server.

iCloud sync

If enabled, settings such as playlists and favorites sync between your devices through your personal iCloud account (Apple CloudKit, private database). This data is governed by Apple's privacy terms, is encrypted in transit, and is accessible only to your Apple ID — not to us.

Casting (Google Cast)

On iPhone and iPad, the Cast button uses Google's Cast SDK to discover and stream to Chromecast and Cast-enabled TVs on your network. While the app is running, that SDK may collect limited device identifiers, app-usage, and diagnostic data for its own functionality, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. That data is handled by Google and is not linked to your identity by us — we never receive it. Casting and the Cast SDK are not present in the Apple TV app.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts or sign-ups.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting that we operate. (Google Cast, used only when you cast from an iPhone or iPad, is the one third-party component that collects data — see "Casting" above.)
  • No advertising and no cross-app tracking.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information — we never possess it in the first place.

Network connections the apps make

The apps connect only to: (1) the playlist, stream, and EPG sources you configure; (2) your own GiVo Server on your local network, if paired; (3) Apple's iCloud, if you enable sync; (4) GitHub, when you download GiVo Server updates from this website; and (5) on iPhone and iPad, Google Cast and your Cast-enabled TV, when you cast. Your content providers can see the requests your device makes to them, exactly as they would with any player — their privacy practices are their own.

This website

givostreamapp.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We run no analytics and set no cookies. GitHub may log standard web-server request data (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Support email

If you email support@givostreamapp.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to reply, and we don't add it to any mailing list.

Children

The apps are not directed at children and collect no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated effective date. Since the apps collect nothing, changes would most likely reflect new optional features (and would say so plainly).
