Everything a player should have done years ago

No gimmicks — just the features you actually use, engineered to stay fast with thousands of streams.

⏯️

Pause & rewind live streams

Every stream gets a timeshift buffer the moment you tune in. Pause for the doorbell, scrub back for the replay, snap back to the live edge — with frame-accurate seeking, not guesswork.

A DVR you actually own

The GiVo Server app turns your Mac or Windows PC into your personal DVR. One-tap record, scheduled recordings with pre/post padding, and limits you control — recordings that are yours to keep.

📱

Every device, one DVR

Pair your iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV with the same server. Set a recording from the couch, watch it on the train. Recording badges and schedules stay in sync everywhere.

⏭️

Commercial skip

Your GiVo Server finds the ad breaks in every finished recording automatically. They appear as orange segments on the timeline — jump past a break with one tap, or turn on auto-skip and never see it at all.

🗓️

A guide that keeps up

A beautiful program grid that scrolls smoothly through thousands of streams, with search, favorites, and up to 7 days of guide data — you choose how much.

🎬

Movies & series, on demand

Browse your provider's full on-demand catalog — posters, plots, cast, and trailers. Resume where you left off, keep a watchlist, and save any title to your DVR server. Add a TMDB key for even richer artwork and details.

Multiview: several channels at once

Put up to four live channels on screen together in grid or spotlight layouts. Tap any tile to change its channel, switch the audio, go fullscreen, or rearrange — made for game day. On Apple TV, iPhone, and iPad.

⏪

Catchup, done right

Providers' catchup archives play with instant, frame-accurate scrubbing — GiVo Stream buffers the program in the background while you watch, so skipping around feels effortless. Save any replay to your DVR to keep it permanently.

📂

Your playlists, as they are

Standard M3U playlists and Xtream Codes logins, with XMLTV guide data, per-playlist auto-refresh, and instant switching between sources.

📡

AirPlay & Chromecast

Send what you're watching to any big screen. Lock-screen and Control Center controls work exactly like you'd expect.

🛡️

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your playlists and credentials stay on your devices, and GiVo Server keeps your recordings on your own Mac or PC. Read the privacy policy.

⚙️

Honest engineering

Streams open in a couple of seconds. Recordings survive provider hiccups with automatic reconnect and repair. When something's wrong, the app tells you what — no silent spinners.

GiVo Server

The DVR companion. Install it on a computer that stays home, pair once, and every device gets recording, scheduling, and catchup.

GiVo Server for macOS

macOS 13 Ventura or later · Universal (Apple silicon & Intel)

Download for Mac

Version 1.0 (build 20)

Notarized by Apple. Open the DMG and drag GiVo Server to Applications — it lives in your menu bar.

GiVo Server for Windows

Windows 10 / 11 · 64-bit

Download for Windows

Version 1.0 (build 20)

Installer sets up the firewall and Start menu for you. If SmartScreen warns, choose “More info” → “Run anyway” (unsigned during pre-release).

  1. Install and launch. GiVo Server lives in the menu bar (Mac) or system tray (Windows) and starts its DVR engine automatically.
  2. Pair your devices. In GiVo Stream, open DVR → DVR Server. Your server is discovered automatically — or add it by IP — then enter the pairing code from the server's menu. Pairing is one-time per device.
  3. Record anything. Tap record in the guide, watch from any paired device, and manage everything right from the app.

Questions, answered

Does GiVo Stream include any content?

No. GiVo Stream and GiVo Server are media player applications — they don't provide, include, or sell any channels, streams, or other content. You add your own playlist sources, and you're responsible for having the right to access and record what you watch.

Where do my recordings live?

GiVo Server for macOS and Windows stores recordings on the computer it runs on, and every paired device streams them from there.

Do I need the server to use the app?

No — playback, the guide, and pause/rewind work on their own. The server adds recording, scheduling, and watching your recordings from every paired device.

What do I need to run GiVo Server?

Any Mac on macOS 13 Ventura or later (Apple silicon or Intel), or a 64-bit Windows 10/11 PC, on the same network as your devices. It's a lightweight menu-bar/tray app — fine to run on the computer you already use.
