Trakt sign-in

You can close this page and return to GiVo Stream.

How signing in works

GiVo Stream connects to your Trakt account with Trakt's device sign-in. The app shows a short code, you enter it at trakt.tv/activate and approve GiVo Stream there, and the app picks up the approval on its own. No password is ever typed into GiVo Stream, and nothing is collected on this page.

What the connection is used for

Once connected, the app can record what you watch to your Trakt history, keep your ratings, watchlist and lists in sync, and show your Trakt lists inside the app. You can disconnect at any time from Settings → Connected Services in GiVo Stream, or by revoking GiVo Stream under your Trakt connected apps.
